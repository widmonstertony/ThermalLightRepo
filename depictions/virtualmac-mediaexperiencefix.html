VirtualMac Audio Stability Fix 1.0.1

Preserves VirtualMac microphone input and speaker output while preventing the iPadOS 16.1 MediaExperience/Now Playing teardown race that can terminate the virtual machine.

Compatibility

Built for iOS/iPadOS 16 rootless jailbreak environments. It expects VirtualMac's compatibility library at its standard installation path.

Installation: close VirtualMac before installing or upgrading, then launch it again after installation. Removing the package restores the backed-up original compatibility library.

中文说明

在保留 VirtualMac 麦克风输入和扬声器输出的同时，修复 iPadOS 16.1 中 MediaExperience/正在播放模块销毁竞态引起的虚拟机崩溃。安装或升级前请关闭 VirtualMac； 卸载插件时会恢复已备份的原始兼容库。
