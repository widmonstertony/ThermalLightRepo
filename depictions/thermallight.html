ThermalLightControl 3.3.0

Prevents thermal brightness caps on iPadOS 16 rootless jailbreaks while preserving CPU/GPU throttling, temperature monitoring, thermal warnings, watchdog, and emergency shutdown.

What's new

Version 3.3.0 uses the real user-requested brightness when sanitizing compact AABC display updates, preventing the brightness cap from returning when a game becomes active.

Compatibility

Verified on iPadOS 16.1 with a rootless jailbreak and ElleKit. Published support is limited to iOS/iPadOS 16.

Controls

The Settings app contains a bilingual on/off switch. The command-line utility supports on, off, status, and check.

Warning: sustained high brightness at elevated temperatures can increase power consumption, display wear, and overheating risk. Monitor device temperature and disable the tweak if the device becomes unusually hot.
