ThermalLightControl 3.3.0

Prevents thermal brightness caps on iPadOS 16 while preserving CPU/GPU throttling, temperature monitoring, warnings, watchdog, and emergency shutdown.

Safety: sustained high brightness at elevated temperatures increases power consumption, display wear, and overheating risk. Monitor device temperature and disable the tweak if the device becomes unusually hot.

VirtualMac Audio Stability Fix 1.0.1

Preserves VirtualMac microphone and speaker support while preventing the iPadOS 16.1 MediaExperience/Now Playing teardown race.
